## Name

HOMIZI

## Short description

Find a home or a flatmate in Indian cities. Contact stays hidden until both people agree.

## Full description

HOMIZI helps tenants and hosts look for rentals and flatmates.

- Explore homes in Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Delhi NCR, with rent in rupees.
- Say what you need: city, locality, budget, and how many people. The form does not ask for gender, religion, caste, or age.
- Like a home. A mutual match opens a chat. A pass does not remove a match that already exists.
- Phone numbers and email addresses stay out of chat. A visit is a time, not a street address.
- Share your own contact for one match, or stop sharing. You do not see the other person's contact unless they have shared it.
- Save homes, edit your profile, and read match, message, and visit notices in the inbox.
- Report or block from the flag icon. Reports are stored. A staffed review queue is not connected yet.

The current build can run in demo mode on the device. Identity check is a mock. HOMIZI does not collect Aadhaar numbers and does not take payments in the app.

## What this draft must not claim

- That identity is verified with a government id.
- That a moderator is reviewing reports.
- That rent can be paid inside the app.
- That push notifications are delivered.
- That the privacy policy or terms are final. Those drafts are in `docs/legal/` and are marked for legal review.
