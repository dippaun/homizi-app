## Using the app

HOMIZI is a way to look for a rental or a flatmate. An account can be a tenant, a host, or both. Browsing homes requires sign-in.

You are responsible for what you put in a profile, a requirement, a listing, and a chat message. Do not put a phone number, an email address, or a street address in chat. Do not upload a photo you do not have the right to share.

A like can become a mutual match. A pass does not undo a match that already exists. Contact details stay hidden until the other person has shared them for that match. You can stop sharing your own contact.

## What HOMIZI is not, in this build

- It is not an identity-verification service. A sandbox check can store a status, a provider reference, and the last 4 characters. It does not confirm an Aadhaar number or a face, and it does not store either.
- It is not a payment service. Rent and deposits shown on a listing are descriptions. A test-mode payment can be simulated, and that path does not collect money.
- It is not a staffed moderation service. A report is stored and a block is enforced. A staff account can review the queue, hide a listing, or suspend an account. Nobody is named or seeded as that account.
- Push stays off until Firebase is compiled in. Notices stay in the in-app inbox either way.
- A home-service form asks a HOMIZI partner to contact you. It is not a confirmed booking.

## Demo mode

When no project URL and public key are set, the app keeps data on the device and does not claim that a server published a listing or a requirement.

## Ending an account

Deleting an account from Settings removes the profile, listings, photos, messages, and contact row on this device or on the connected local server. It does not call a payment provider, an identity vendor, or a production backup. That limitation is part of this draft, not a promise about a future production deletion program.
