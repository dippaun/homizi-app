## Full description

HOMIZI helps tenants and hosts look for rentals and flatmates in Indian cities.

- Explore homes in Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Delhi NCR. Rent is shown in rupees. Cards show a locality and a city, not a street address.
- Say what you need: city, locality, budget, occupancy, and a move-in date. The form does not ask for gender, religion, caste, or age.
- Like a home. A mutual match opens a chat. A pass does not remove a match that already exists.
- Phone numbers and email addresses stay out of chat. A visit is a time, not a street address.
- Share your own contact for one match, or stop sharing. You do not see the other person's contact unless they have shared it.
- Save homes, edit a profile, and read match, message, and visit notices in the inbox. Those notices are not push notifications.
- Report or block from the flag icon. A staff account can review the queue. No moderator is named in this build.
- Product analytics can be turned off in Settings. Crash reports stay off unless the build was compiled with a Sentry DSN, which this repository does not contain.

The app can run in demo mode on the device when no Supabase project is configured. A connected build uses the Supabase project it was compiled with. The staging project is in Mumbai.

Identity check is a mock. HOMIZI does not collect Aadhaar numbers, a face scan, or payments.
